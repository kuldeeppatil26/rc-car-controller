These are Nordic UART Service UUIDs. They MUST exactly match the BLE Service and Characteristics in your ESP32 firmware.
Discovery troubleshooting: If the ESP32 does not appear when Service filtering is ON, turn filtering OFF and select the device manually. If it appears as "Unknown or Unsupported Device", select it — that label can be normal for a custom ESP32 BLE GATT device. If connection then reports "Service not found", the ESP32 firmware UUIDs do not match these values.
